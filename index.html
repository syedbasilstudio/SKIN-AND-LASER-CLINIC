Welcome

A clinic built around the person in the chair.

The Skin & Laser Clinic is an established clinic on High Cross Road in Poulton-le-Fylde, where skin, laser and aesthetic treatments are planned one person at a time. Nothing here is off the shelf: the treatment, the areas covered and the number of sessions are worked out with you, not decided for you.

If a treatment isn't right for you, the clinic will say so — that honesty is the reason clients keep coming back and bringing their friends.

    Where To Begin

    Featured treatments

    If you're not sure which treatment to ask about first, these are the ones clients raise most often. Each page of prices sets out exactly what's listed — and the clinic advises on suitability at your consultation rather than promising an outcome.

    Laser

    Laser Hair Removal

    One of the clinic's most established treatments, delivered the same careful way each time: a consultation and patch test, a plan for the areas you want covered, then sessions spaced to suit your skin and hair growth.

    Courses of eight sessions, single sessions and combination areas are all listed, so you can start with one area and add others later.

    • Eight-session courses for women and men, with separate price lists
    • Single sessions and top-ups, including combination areas
    • New clients book a consultation and patch test first
    View laser hair removal pricesBook Appointment

    HIFU

    HIFU Skin Tightening

    Focus Dual Fusion HIFU is listed for the eyes and forehead, the neck, the upper or lower face, the full face and neck, and for small and large body areas.

    A free 15-minute consultation establishes which areas are appropriate for you and how many sessions that would involve — the clinic decides that with you, not for you.

    • Free 15-minute consultation
    • Face, neck and body areas priced separately
    • Suitability confirmed by the clinic at your consultation
    View HIFU pricesBook Appointment

    Brows & Lashes

    Brows & Lashes

    Semi-permanent makeup is listed as Ombré brows and powder brows, with a top-up within four to six weeks. Alongside them sit brow waxing, tinting and lamination, lash lifts and the Korean lash lift.

    These are the quicker appointments in the clinic's menu — precise, gentle and finished in a single visit.

    • Consultation and patch test listed as free
    • Ombré and powder brows — £150 each
    • Top-up within 4–6 weeks — £50
    View brows & lashes pricesBook Appointment

    Skin

    Radiofrequency & Microneedling

    Radiofrequency is listed for the full face, the face and neck, the body and for hair rejuvenation. Microneedling is available as an express treatment for the face, with hyaluronic acid, or combined with polynucleotides.

    The clinic confirms which of these suits your skin, and how many sessions that would take.

    • RF full face — £200 · RF full face & neck — £275
    • Microneedling express — £50 · with hyaluronic — £80
    • Microneedling & polynucleotides — £120
    View skin treatment pricesBook Appointment

    Also At The Clinic

    Laser Tattoo Removal

    Picosecond laser tattoo removal, priced by size — extra small at 5 × 5 cm through to extra large at 30 × 30 cm, with multiple tattoos and microblading removal listed separately. The consultation and patch test is free, 15 minutes.
